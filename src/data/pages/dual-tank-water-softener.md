### What Is a Dual Tank Water Softener System?

A dual tank water softener is a twin-tank system in which two resin tanks share the softening work. While Tank A softens the water you use, Tank B regenerates, then the roles switch. A single-tank softener pauses softening during regeneration and sends [hard water](/water-softener-guides/hard-water-albuquerque-nm/) to the house until the cycle ends. A dual tank system removes that gap, and many designs regenerate only when a tank is actually exhausted, which uses salt and water in line with real demand.

### Benefits for High-Usage Albuquerque Homes

ABCWUA's supply is locally pumped groundwater blended with treated San Juan-Chama surface water, and hard water is a daily fact for local households. ABCWUA's 2023 water quality report put the city average hardness at 8.44 grains per gallon, about 144 mg/L, which the U.S. Geological Survey rates as hard. Dual tank systems help most where water use is high:

- **Large families** with several showers, loads of laundry and a dishwasher running in the same hours.
- **Homes with multiple bathrooms, pools or irrigation draws** that put heavy demand on a single tank.
- **Households with night-time or early-morning peaks**, since regeneration no longer interrupts softened water.

### Commercial Dual Tank Systems

Businesses that need soft water around the clock benefit most from two tanks. Restaurants, hotels, laundries and car washes run equipment where even a short stretch of hard water causes scale in boilers, steamers and spray nozzles. A commercial dual tank system is sized to peak flow rate, not household size.

### Cost and Installation

Cost depends on tank capacity, the number of units, plumbing changes and whether the install location already has a drain and power. Commercial pricing is quoted after a site visit. Installation follows the same steps as any softener: a free consultation, a [water test](/water-quality-testing/) and [sizing](/water-softener-guides/water-softener-size-guide/), professional installation with a bypass valve, and support afterward.

> **Not a good fit if...** you live in a small household with modest water use. A properly sized single tank costs less and softens fully.
