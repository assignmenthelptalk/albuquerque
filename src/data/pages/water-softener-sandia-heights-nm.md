## Why Sandia Heights Residents Need a Hardness Test

The New Mexico Public Regulation Commission lists Sandia Peak Utility's water service as covering Sandia Heights and Primrose Pointe, with its office at #120 Tramway Loop NE in Albuquerque. That makes Sandia Heights a different water system from the one ABCWUA reports on. ABCWUA's 2023 report gave a city average of 8.44 grains per gallon, and that number belongs to ABCWUA's customers, not to Sandia Peak Utility's.

We found no published hardness figure for Sandia Peak Utility, so we do not quote one. A free test at your tap gives the number, and you can ask the utility for its most recent water quality report, which a public water system produces each year.

## About Sandia Heights Water Quality

Sandia Peak Utility produces its own water quality reporting, separate from ABCWUA's. If a test at your tap shows hardness above 120 mg/L, about 7 grains per gallon, the U.S. Geological Survey scale rates the water as hard and a softener makes sense.
