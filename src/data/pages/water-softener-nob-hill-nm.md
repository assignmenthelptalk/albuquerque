## Why Nob Hill Residents Need a Water Softener

Builders had constructed about 80 percent of Nob Hill before World War II, on additions platted from 1916 to 1931, including Granada Heights in 1925, College View in 1926 and Mesa Grande in 1931. A home of that age can have original, repaired or fully replaced plumbing, so a free test and a look at the pipes decide what we recommend. Water has a long history here: a house at 319 Carlisle SE, built in 1916, was constructed around the former water tank of the neighborhood's developer, Col. D. K. B. Sellers.

ABCWUA's 2023 water quality report put the city average hardness at 8.44 grains per gallon, about 144 mg/L, within a city range of 2.09 to 11.72 grains per gallon, and the U.S. Geological Survey rates 121 to 180 mg/L as hard. At 8.44 gpg, a household of four using 320 gallons a day removes about 2,700 grains daily, so a 24,000-grain salt-based softener regenerates about every 9 days.

## About Nob Hill Water Quality

ABCWUA blends locally pumped groundwater with treated San Juan-Chama surface water, and its 2025 report says groundwater needs little treatment other than chlorination. Its 2023 distribution monitoring measured free chlorine at a city average of 0.9 mg/L and total dissolved solids at a city average of 291 ppm. ABCWUA publishes results by distribution zone, and its figures are citywide or by zone, so a test at your tap gives the number for your home.
