## What Do Albuquerque Homeowners Need to Know About Hard Water?

Three questions cover the basics: what hard water is, why Albuquerque water is hard and how hard it measures.

<div class="svc-grid">

<div class="svc-card">

### What Is Hard Water?

Hard water is water with a high concentration of dissolved calcium and magnesium. These minerals dissolve as water moves through rock and soil, and they stay in the water until something removes them. Hardness is measured in milligrams per liter (mg/L) of calcium carbonate or in grains per gallon (gpg), where 1 gpg equals about 17.1 mg/L. The U.S. Geological Survey rates water under 60 mg/L as soft, 61 to 120 as moderately hard, 121 to 180 as hard and over 180 as very hard.

</div>

![Why Is Albuquerque Water Hard? Albuquerque NM](/placeholders/card.svg)

### Why Is Albuquerque Water Hard?

Albuquerque water is hard because of the rock and sediment the water moves through. According to ABCWUA, treated surface water and groundwater from both sides of the river have been blended and served throughout its system since December 2008, and depending on the sources and blend the hardness ranges from moderately hard to hard. ABCWUA's 2025 water quality report says customers rely on locally pumped groundwater plus surface water imported from the Colorado River basin through the San Juan-Chama Project. A 1988 U.S. Geological Survey report by Scott K. Anderholm entitled “Ground-Water Geochemistry of the Albuquerque-Belen Basin, Central New Mexico” found that the chemistry of the water flowing into the basin's principal aquifer has the largest effect on the water quality found in different parts of the basin, with calcium and bicarbonate the dominant ions on the eastern side, while in a large area west of Albuquerque sodium dominates, probably because calcium and magnesium exchange for sodium. Water that has moved through rock and sediment picks up calcium and magnesium on the way.

</div>

### How Hard Is Albuquerque Water?

ABCWUA's 2023 water quality report put the city average hardness at 8.44 grains per gallon, about 144 mg/L, which the U.S. Geological Survey rates as hard. The same report gave a city range of 2.09 to 11.72 grains per gallon, a city average of 48.64 ppm calcium and 5.55 ppm magnesium, the two minerals that make up hardness. Readings vary across the city and through the year because the mix of surface water and groundwater changes, so the number at your tap can differ. A free test at your address gives the figure that matters for choosing equipment.

### What Hard Water Does to a Home

- **Appliances:** Scale builds inside water heaters, dishwashers and washing machines. A heater coated in scale works harder to heat the same water.
- **Pipes and fixtures:** Mineral deposits narrow pipes, clog showerheads and leave white crust on faucets.
- **Skin and hair:** Hard water reacts with soap to form a film that leaves skin dry and hair dull.
- **Laundry:** Minerals stiffen fabric, dull colors and reduce how well detergent cleans.
- **Dishes and glass:** Spots and cloudy film appear after the dishwasher runs.

Hard water is a quality and comfort problem. ABCWUA's reports list results for regulated contaminants separately, and hardness is not a health-based limit.

### Solutions for Hard Water

Three options reduce the effects of hard water:

1. **A water softener** removes calcium and magnesium through ion exchange and gives true soft water. [Water Softener Installation in Albuquerque, NM](/water-softener-installation/) is the most complete fix for the whole house.
2. **A conditioner or descaler** changes how minerals form scale without removing them. It reduces scale with less maintenance but does not make water feel soft.
3. **Point-of-use treatment**, such as a shower filter or an under-sink system, improves one tap and leaves the rest of the house untreated.

Most Albuquerque homeowners choose a [salt-based](/water-softener-guides/salt-vs-salt-free-water-softener/) softener when hardness is the main complaint. A test first confirms hardness and checks for iron, which affects [sizing](/water-softener-guides/water-softener-size-guide/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

<div class="faq-list">

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Is Albuquerque water safe to drink?</summary>

ABCWUA's water quality reports list results against federal limits. Hardness is a quality issue, not a health limit, and the reports cover regulated contaminants separately.

</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Does a water softener remove minerals I need?</summary>

A softener swaps calcium and magnesium for sodium or potassium. Food supplies most of your minerals, and drinking water is a minor source.

</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Why does my glassware spot even with a rinse aid?</summary>

Dissolved minerals dry on glass as white spots. Softened water stops the spotting at the source.

</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Can hard water damage my water heater?</summary>

Scale insulates the heating element or tank bottom, which makes the heater work harder and shortens its life. Flushing helps, and softened water prevents most of the buildup.

</details>

<summary>Do homes on well water have harder water?</summary>

Often yes, since private wells draw directly from the aquifer. A test shows the hardness and checks for iron and other minerals.
