## What Water Conditioning Options Are Available in Albuquerque, NM?

Five options treat scale and [hard water](/water-softener-guides/hard-water-albuquerque-nm/): [salt-free conditioners](/salt-free-water-softener-systems/), potassium chloride softener systems, hard water treatment, limescale treatment and scale inhibitor systems.

### Salt-Free Water Conditioners

A salt-free water conditioner changes how hardness minerals behave so they form less scale, and it leaves the minerals in the water. It needs no salt and no drain. It suits homeowners who want scale protection with minimal upkeep. Our salt-free page covers template assisted crystallization and the other methods in detail.

</div>

<div class="svc-card">


![Potassium Chloride Water Softener Systems Albuquerque NM](/placeholders/card.svg)

### Potassium Chloride Water Softener Systems

A potassium chloride water softener works like a standard softener, using ion exchange to remove calcium and magnesium, but it regenerates with potassium chloride instead of sodium chloride. The result is softened water without added sodium. Potassium chloride costs more per bag than salt, so running costs run higher. It suits households that limit sodium or follow a doctor's guidance on salt.

</div>

### Hard Water Treatment Service

A hard water treatment service starts with testing and ends with the right equipment. The options are a softener that removes hardness, a conditioner that reduces scale or a scale inhibitor that protects specific equipment. ABCWUA's supply is locally pumped groundwater blended with treated San Juan-Chama surface water. ABCWUA's 2023 water quality report put the city average hardness at 8.44 grains per gallon, about 144 mg/L, which the U.S. Geological Survey rates as hard.

</div>

<div class="svc-card">

### Limescale Treatment Service

Limescale is the hard, chalky deposit that builds up inside water heaters, kettles, showerheads and pipes. Our limescale treatment service removes existing buildup from fixtures and equipment and then adds a system to slow its return. Water heaters with heavy scale lose efficiency, so treatment protects energy use as well as hardware.

</div>

<div class="svc-card">


![Scale Inhibitor System Installation Albuquerque NM](/placeholders/card.svg)

### Scale Inhibitor System Installation

A scale inhibitor system feeds a small, controlled dose of food-grade polyphosphate into the water. The polyphosphate coats pipe and heater surfaces so scale clings less. Inhibitors are common ahead of water heaters and commercial equipment where full softening is not needed.

</div>

### Water Conditioner vs. Softener: What Albuquerque Homes Need

A softener removes hardness. A conditioner reduces scale without removing it.

- **Pick a softener** for very hard water, soft-feeling water at the tap and less spotting.
- **Pick a conditioner** for low maintenance, no salt and scale protection.
- **Pick an inhibitor** to protect a water heater or one piece of equipment.

For the full softener range, see the [Water Treatment Services in Albuquerque, NM](/water-treatment-and-testing/) overview.

> **Not a good fit if...** your water is very hard and you want true soft water, or your well carries iron. A conditioner will not fix either.
